# Video Scripts — 10 Shot-by-Shot (phone-ready)

Filming notes for ALL formats: window light only · film vertical 9:16 · natural Hindi (re-take any line that sounds "ad-like") · keep take 4 of every scene — monotone take 4s are often the winners.

## F1 — "Pajabi war" (15s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Phone POV messy shelf, a deep breath | VO: "Har subah wardrobe ke saath fight." |
| 3–8 | Hands snap Rachna box open (one motion) | Text: "Saans le." SFX: snap |
| 8–12 | 12 shirts in, box closes, shelf fits | VO: "Rachna. 30 seconds mein." |
| 12–15 | End card: box + price | "Buy 2 = ₹1,299. Free shipping ₹999+." |

## F2 — "Weight" (15s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | You climb on the box, camera low | Text: "Mera weight test." |
| 3–10 | Standing, box holds, small smile | VO: "60 kilo. Darr nahi laga." |
| 10–15 | Pile of clothes cut-in, box stacks | Text: "Reinforced corners. 300D fabric." |

## F3 — "Price melody" (15s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | You holding one box, deadpan | "₹999 wali cheez, ₹699 mein." |
| 4–9 | Pull second box into frame | "Do lein toh ₹1,299. Ek free feel karo." |
| 9–15 | Countdown sticker + CTA | "Aaj raat 12 baje khatam." |

## F4 — "15s aadmi kam, demo zyada" (15s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Hands only: fold, click, stack | No words. Music. |
| 5–12 | Wardrobe closes on organized shelf | Text: "No screws. No tools." |
| 12–15 | Price + link | "₹699 (MRP ₹999)" |

## F5 — "30-second story" (30s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | Talking to camera, real messy room behind | "Ye mera hi room tha. Video calls pe sharam aati thi." |
| 6–14 | Hands assemble → load boxes → pair shelf | VO: "Fir Rachna aayi. No tools. No mess." |
| 14–24 | B-roll: 2 days later, neat room, guests clip | VO: "Ab mummy bhi proud, friends bhi." |
| 24–30 | Price + guarantee card | "7-day refund. Risk mera." |

## F6 — "Comparison abuse" (30s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | Split: cheap plastic box week-1 vs week-2 (collapsed) | "Ye ₹400 wali. Week 2 mein bas naam." |
| 8–16 | Rachna box week-1 vs week-6 (perfect) | "Ye Rachna. 6 hafte, wahi standing." |
| 16–24 | Price panels side by side | "Same deal. Now don't get fooled twice." |
| 24–30 | End card, link | "Buy 2 @ ₹1,299." |

## F7 — "Guide" (30s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | 3 boxes laid out | "3 sizes, 3 jagahein." |
| 5–14 | Kitchen demo (small box, tins/meds) | "Kitchen: masale, dawai, tins." |
| 14–22 | Wardrobe demo (medium/large, folded clothes) | "Wardrobe: jeans → kurta sets." |
| 22–30 | Under-bed / car demo | "Under-bed. Car. Ghar ke har kone." |

## F8 — "Objection killer" (30s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | Low light, sincere, direct cam | "Naya brand hai. Main jaanta hoon kaise lagta hai." |
| 6–14 | Live unboxing (imperfect, real-time) | "Dekh lo — packaging hi bolti hai." |
| 14–24 | You testing box with body weight | "7 din. Refund. Label free. Risk mera." |
| 24–30 | Phone with a smile, gesture | "Aap bas ek baar try karo. Deal?" |

## U9 — UGC "Sister" (45–60s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Handheld chaos + gentle laugh | "Maine ye isliye LIYA kyunki mera room change ho gaya, ad dikhane ke liye nahi." |
| 10–35 | 3 use cases, real time | Give her 3 talking points only, never a word-for-word script |
| 35–55 | Honest verdict + box close-up | Her words |
| CTA | End card | "Aapke room ka bhi time aa gaya." |

## U10 — UGC "Mom test" (40s)
| Time | Visual | Audio / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | Mom voice: "Online order karke dekho, kya milega." | Genuine generational scepticism |
| 8–30 | Unbox → assemble → mom picks it up, squeezes, nods | The nod = the money shot, hold it 2s |
| 30–40 | Mom verdict in her language + end card | "Baap re, sahi hai." |

## Capture checklist per shoot
- 3 boxes, 2 towels pile, 1 phone tripod/RAM stick
- 20 min golden-hour window light
- 2 takes per scene (chalo-chalo take + serious take)
- Clothes for demo: 12 t-shirts, 3 jeans, 1 blanket
- White wall + same spot for all before/after
