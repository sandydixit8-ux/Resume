# रचना · Rachna Home — Brand Guide

## Name
रचना / **Rachna** ("to create, to arrange")

## Taglines
| Use | Text |
|---|---|
| Hero | "Ghar, saaf-suthra aur sundar." |
| English alt | "Beautifully organised. Effortlessly home." |
| Product line | "Your wardrobe, organised in 30 seconds." |

## Brand story (40 words)
Rachna started in a small rented room whose cupboard had won. Rs 400 "storage hacks"
from marketplaces folded in two weeks — so we tested fabrics, corner joints and real
Indian shelf sizes until one box survived. Rachna makes honest storage for real homes
and real budgets.

## Personality
The sister who has life together — practical, warm, zero-bullshit, lightly funny.
Never corporate. Never shouty.

## Brand promise
Honest product. Fast delivery. Refund if we disappoint.
(Repeated on every page and ad — it is the prepaid trust engine.)

## Tone of voice
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short sentences; console-ready | Jargon or "cutting-edge" |
| Natural Hindi mixed in | Fake-pretentious English |
| Admit limits ("screens vary") | Guarantee perfection |
| Speak to one woman, not a crowd | "Dear customers" |

## Logo direction
Wordmark "rach" + woven-basket motif icon. Clean rounded sans (Poppins bold),
one accent colour, generous whitespace. No gradients, no 3D.

## Colour palette
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Terracotta | `#B94B2A` | Primary CTA, logo accent |
| Deep Green | `#24443C` | Text, secondary buttons, badges |
| Cream | `#F7F1E7` | Backgrounds, packaging |
| Gold | `#D9A441` | Urgency, ratings, highlights |
| Charcoal | `#2B2B28` | Body text |

## Typography
- Headings: Poppins (600–800 weight)
- Body: Inter
- Largest type on any page = the promise, not the logo.

## Packaging concept
Kraft corrugated box (eco) → tissue paper → product in branded cotton dust bag →
thank-you card with WhatsApp QR → sticker with MRP, GSTIN, "Made in India".
Unboxing must be photo-worthy — first 10 customers will post it for free.

- Trademark search: https://ipindia.gov.in
- Domain: check `rachna.in` + `iambecause...` variants on a registrar
- Instagram / Facebook / WhatsApp Business handles: must be free
- If taken → pick next name from master plan list and adapt this guide (do not
  fall in love with a name you can't own).
