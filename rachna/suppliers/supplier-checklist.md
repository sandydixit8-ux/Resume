## Red flags (walk away)
- No GSTIN / refuses invoice
- Only "stock photos," no real product video
- "Sell it online karo hamara brand laga do" (they want you to retail THEIR brand)
- Sample cost above ₹300/set
- Bulk price below ₹180/set → almost certainly fabric GSM < 240
- Won't share exact collapsed dimensions

## Sample-test routine (same day samples arrive)
1. Weigh + measure folded and assembled → re-run shipping math
2. Load test: 15 kg books 4 hours → corners sag?
3. Zip/velcro cycle test ×50
4. Fabric GSM feel check (240+ = pass)
5. Photograph everything — this is your first creative material
6. Keep one sample for content, two for ads
