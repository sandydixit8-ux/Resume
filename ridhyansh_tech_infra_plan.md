4. DELIVERY APPROACH (6-PHASE PROCESS)
================================================================================

Our structured delivery process keeps implementation predictable and reduces rework.

01  DISCOVERY   (2-3 days)
    • Understand process, systems, users, data and success metrics
    • Map workflow logic, integrations, exceptions and approvals

02  DESIGN      (2-3 days)
    • Map workflow logic, integrations, exceptions and approvals
    • Define triggers, business rules, and success criteria

03  BUILD       (3-5 days)
    • Configure integrations, automation, AI components and controls
    • Set up n8n environment, workflows, and connections

04  TEST        (2-3 days)
    • Validate normal paths, edge cases, errors and outputs
    • Quality assurance and user acceptance testing

05  DEPLOY      (2-3 days)
    • Release to production with documentation and handover
    • Training for internal team

06  OPTIMISE    (Ongoing)
    • Review performance and extend automation where value is proven
    • Monthly review and improvement recommendations

Typical implementation: 5-10 business days for a defined workflow scope;
complex integrations may require additional time.
